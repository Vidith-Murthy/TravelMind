It is important to pick safe places to stay, especially when traveling alone. Safety features and accessibility can be different everywhere.

Before travel, research hotels or lodgings carefully, check information about the destination, and read online reviews to prepare for potential safety risks. Call the hotel before booking if special facilities are needed and confirm they are available. Ask whether the property has working elevators, ramps, and other accessibility features. Ask whether rooms have carbon monoxide detectors. Book accommodations before traveling and have backup options ready in case a location must be changed.

When selecting safe and accessible accommodations, consider proximity to public transportation and important services, crime rates near the lodging area, and safety measures such as 24-hour lobby service, gates, or security guards.

During a stay, guests should be able to contact the front desk about suspicious activity. Guests should know the emergency exit plan posted in the room, find the closest emergency exit, and learn the emergency route after arrival.

Publisher: Global Sustainable Tourism Council (GSTC)

The Global Sustainable Tourism Council Standards provide a common understanding of sustainable tourism and are the minimum that any tourism business should aspire to reach. They are organised around four main themes: effective sustainability planning; maximising social and economic benefits for the local community; enhancing cultural heritage; and reducing negative impacts to the environment. They apply to the entire tourism industry.

The GSTC Industry Standard evolved into two distinct standards in 2025: the GSTC Hotel Standard and the GSTC Tour Operator Standard. The Hotel Standard can serve as a basis for sustainability certification, guidelines for businesses to become more sustainable, market and supplier guidance, consumer information, a baseline for voluntary programmes, a starting point for governmental and private-sector tourism requirements, and education and training.

The standards indicate what should be done, not how to do it or whether a goal has been achieved. Performance indicators, educational materials, and implementation tools complement the standards. GSTC also identifies standards for tour operators, destinations, MICE, attractions, and food and beverage businesses in tourism.

Commercial use of the GSTC Standards may be subject to terms set by GSTC; confirm current licensing and applicability before using the standards in a commercial product.
